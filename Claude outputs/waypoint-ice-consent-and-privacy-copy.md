## 2. Privacy policy — Emergency (ICE) & Location section

*Add as a section in the Waypoint privacy policy.*

### Emergency Information and Location Data

**What we collect.** For certain organized events, we collect emergency information you choose to provide when you register: an emergency contact (name, phone, relationship), and — to help responders — your blood type and known allergies. We also process your real-time and historical GPS location for the events you participate in, sourced from your satellite beacon or device.

**Why we collect it, and our legal basis.** We use emergency information and location solely to (a) display live tracking for the event and (b) enable emergency response if you or an organizer triggers an SOS. Because blood type and allergies are health-related information and precise location is sensitive, we rely on your **explicit consent**, given at registration, and — in a life-threatening situation — your and others' **vital interests**. We do not use this data for advertising, and **we do not sell personal or location data**.

**Who can see it.** Emergency information is visible only to the **organizer of the event you registered for**, and — during an active SOS — to the emergency responders assisting you. It is never shown on public maps, embeds, or share links, which display only your name, number, class, and position. Every access to your emergency information is logged.

**How long we keep it.** Emergency information is **automatically deleted 7 days after the event ends**. Precise location tracks are purged on the same schedule. The single exception is an **SOS incident record** — if an emergency occurs, we retain a timestamped incident report (including the emergency information as it was at that time) for **up to one year** as a safety and liability record, after which it is deleted.

**Health information and HIPAA.** We treat blood type and allergies as personal health information and protect them accordingly. Waypoint is not a healthcare provider, health plan, or clearinghouse, so this information is **not "Protected Health Information" under HIPAA**, and we do not represent the service as HIPAA-compliant. It is handled as sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws (including the California CPRA and, for EU/UK residents, the GDPR).

**Your choices and rights.** Providing emergency information is optional — you may leave any field blank. You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time, and you may generate a **personal emergency card that stays entirely on your own device** and is never sent to us. To exercise any right, contact admin@chasingwaypoints.com.

**How we protect it.** All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted by role at the database level, secrets are never exposed to browsers, and payment card data is handled entirely by Stripe and never stored by us.
